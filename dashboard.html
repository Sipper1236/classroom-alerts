Reload Classroom tabs

After updating or reloading the extension, reload every open Classroom tab so its page scripts update too. Old or mixed scripts are blocked before their data is accepted. Then scan again.

Data recovery — automation blocked

The original stored data has not been replaced. Export validated records before resetting. Recovery of supported schemas keeps a local backup of the original (including saved secrets); recovered snapshots require Classroom verification and delivery is disabled. Unsupported newer schemas cannot be recovered by this version.

What should I work on?

Recommendations use deadlines and visible status, not AI. Local progress does not submit work to Classroom.

Provisional summaries — verify in Classroom

Visible assignment cards without stable item links are local, unverified observations, not identified tasks. They never enter recommendations or Discord. Identical titles are not merged or matched to assignments. Open the checked course scope and verify details manually. This bounded inventory keeps at most 200 summaries; a fresh scan replaces that page's snapshot, not your identified task records. JSON backups include these snapshots; task CSV does not.

Academic snapshots — local only

Announcements, materials and grades require the separate collection switches in Settings. Only supported visible UI regions are collected; an empty list is not proof that there are no posts or grades. Grades are individual snapshots, never an official course average. No announcement bodies, instructions, files or grades are forwarded to Discord.

Discord notification events

First synchronization does not send one new-item alert per historical task. Re-enabling an event does not replay past changes. After a scheduling gap of at least 30 minutes, multiple eligible missed alerts are combined into one bounded current-state catch-up. Quiet hours, rate limits and spacing still apply. Stale reminder windows are discarded, not replayed; overflow is summarized with a Dashboard notice rather than more messages. Partially delivered digests retain their existing cursor. Pending events expire after 48 hours.

Only a newly entered replacement can be revealed; the saved secret is never loaded into this page.

Messages share assignment titles, course names, deadlines, status and links with Discord and channel members. Use a private channel and school-approved sharing. No grades, private comments, or diagnostics are sent. Keys are stored locally, not in an encrypted vault.

Exact message text preview

Uses saved settings and current records. The digest sample is shown even when delivery is disabled or not due. Queued events are shown only while still valid; later data changes may change the text. Nothing is sent or queued by this preview. Discord Markdown is shown as text; mentions are disabled in the outbound payload.

Local data, backup and retention


Exports contain private tasks, local notes and any locally collected grades/materials, but no settings, webhook, queues or diagnostics. Keep files private. Imports never enable delivery or change settings, and require a fresh Classroom detail check before alerts.

 


 

Retention policy





Automatic cleanup runs at most hourly while the extension is active. Only detail-verified submissions or locally complete tasks with a recorded completion date are eligible; unfinished work and unverified imports are kept regardless of age. Setting completed retention to 0 disables automatic task deletion. Deletion resets affected course baselines to avoid rediscovery alert floods; a later scan may rediscover a deleted task.




 


  


Clearing task history retains course selection, notification preferences, and the saved webhook. Reset removes those too. Neither changes Classroom. A local recovery backup is retained until explicitly deleted with its separate button.
